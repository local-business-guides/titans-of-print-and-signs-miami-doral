Exhibit Doral planning for your next event

A trade show setup is easier to plan when you know what your booth needs to communicate. Use this guide to organize the display pieces and print materials before you order.

Start an Exhibit Doral plan with the booth

Before choosing display pieces, find out what the event space allows. Note the booth dimensions and any setup instructions the organizer has provided. Consider where visitors will approach from, where staff will stand, and which message should catch attention first. These details help you decide what needs to be seen from a distance and what can be picked up at the table.

A trade show package may bring together a backdrop, table cover, apparel, promotional products, and paper materials such as business cards or flyers. Titans of Print and Signs Miami can produce these types of event items. For Exhibit Doral planning, start by listing what you already have and what needs to be created or refreshed. That keeps the discussion focused on your actual booth rather than a generic display.

Build a practical event checklist

Think through how the booth will work during the event, not just how it will look in a design. A backdrop may carry the main brand message, while a handout can provide details visitors might want to keep. A table cover can complete the setup, and staff apparel can help people identify who to speak with. Choose items that have a clear role and fit the space.

Before requesting a quote or production discussion, gather:

  • Event dates and booth dimensions
  • Any display rules or artwork guidance from the organizer
  • Your logo, brand files, and current messaging
  • A list of pieces you need, including quantities where known
  • The date you need materials ready for setup

If the event has a particular audience or offer, include that context. It can guide the message on a backdrop, flyer, or promotional item. If you’re not sure how many items to bring, say so rather than guessing. The shop can discuss your list and the information needed to plan each piece.

Avoid rushed exhibit decisions

Leaving the booth layout until the graphics are underway can lead to materials that compete for attention or don’t suit the available space. Check measurements and event instructions first. Another frequent oversight is treating every printed item as a separate job. Make sure the backdrop, table materials, apparel, and handouts feel like parts of the same presentation by using consistent brand files and messaging.

Review all text before sending artwork, especially contact details, event information, and calls to action. If an item includes a date or a specific promotion, verify it carefully. Keep a simple inventory of what you plan to bring, too. That helps you spot missing pieces while there’s still time to ask about them.

When to call an exhibit print shop

Contact a print shop when you need several booth materials, have questions about display choices, or want help with graphic design. Bring the event details and your current artwork, even if the plan isn’t complete. For Exhibit Doral projects, a clear conversation about space, audience, and timing helps the shop understand what you’re preparing for.

Questions people ask about Exhibit Doral

What materials can I include in a trade show exhibit?

Depending on your booth, you may need a backdrop, table cover, apparel, promotional products, business cards, or flyers. Start with the event space and the job each item should do.

What should I bring when discussing exhibit printing?

Have the booth dimensions, event instructions, artwork, and a list of needed pieces ready if available. Include the date you need materials for the event.

Can the booth materials share one visual style?

Yes, consistent logos, colors, and messaging can help the pieces feel coordinated. Share your brand files and explain the main message you want visitors to remember.
